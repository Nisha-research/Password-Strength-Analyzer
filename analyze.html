Security basics

Why strong passwords matter

A weak password is often the easiest way for attackers to break into accounts.

🔒

Protects your identity

Email, banking, social accounts, and cloud storage all rely on strong passwords.

🛡️

Limits damage

Even one weak account can lead to data loss, fraud, or unauthorized access elsewhere.

Brute force: automated guessing of many password combinations
Dictionary attack: trying common words, names, and leaked passwords
Credential stuffing: reusing stolen passwords across multiple sites

The safest approach is to use a long, unique passphrase or password manager-generated password for each account. This makes attacks much slower and far less likely to succeed.
